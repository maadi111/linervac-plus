## Full Description

**LinerVac+** - Your Complete Pool Automation Control Center

Take full control of your LinerVac+ pool vacuum and water valve automation system right from your smartphone. Whether you're at home or away, manage your pool maintenance with ease.

**Key Features:**

🏊 **Pool Vacuum Control**
- Start and stop your pool vacuum remotely
- Real-time status updates
- Double-tap safety confirmation

💧 **Water Valve Automation**
- Remote water valve control
- Coordinate with vacuum operations
- Instant command execution

📹 **Live Camera Monitoring**
- View real-time camera feed
- Full-screen zoomable view
- Remote camera on/off control

🏠 **Safe Home Shutdown**
- Emergency shutdown for all systems
- One-tap safety feature
- Double-tap protection

📱 **Multi-Unit Support**
- Manage up to 5 units from one app
- Easy switching between units
- Quick QR code or manual binding

🔔 **Smart Notifications**
- Reed switch alerts
- Connection status updates
- Repeating reminders

📊 **Analytics & Tracking**
- Usage statistics
- Power times and operational hours
- Firmware version information

✅ Intuitive interface designed for all skill levels  
✅ Real-time control and monitoring  
✅ Support for multiple pool units  
✅ Offline capability with local storage  

Perfect for pool owners, maintenance professionals, and property managers who want convenient, reliable control of their pool automation systems.
